# Customer-Data-Analysis
# 📊 Customer Purchase Behavior & Revenue Insights

## 🧾 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior and revenue patterns using **Power BI**, supported by **Python** and **SQL** for data processing.  
The goal is to help businesses understand **who their customers are, how they purchase, and which segments drive the most revenue**, enabling data-driven marketing and strategic decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer transactions across **gender, age groups, and product categories**
- Identify **top-performing products** and **high-value customer segments**
- Understand **payment method preferences**
- Generate **actionable business insights** for marketing optimization

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

| Tool | Purpose |
|-----|--------|
| Power BI | Dashboard creation & data visualization |
| Python | Data cleaning, feature engineering (adding new columns) |
| SQL | Initial data extraction and database connection |
| Excel / CSV | Raw dataset storage |

---

## 📈 Key Dashboard Features

### KPIs
- Total Revenue  
- Total Transactions  
- Total Quantity  
- Average Revenue per Transaction  

### Insights Visualized
- Revenue & Transactions by **Gender**
- Revenue & Transactions by **Age Group**
- **Product Category** performance
- **Payment Method** distribution
- **Category-wise payment behavior**

### Interactive Filters
- Year  
- Product Category  
- Gender  

---

## 🔍 Major Insights

- **Females contribute higher revenue per transaction**
- **Age group 26–45** generates the highest revenue and transaction volume
- **Clothing** and **Food & Beverage** are the top-performing categories
- **Cash remains the dominant payment method**, though digital payments are increasing

---

## 🧠 Business Recommendations

- Prioritize marketing campaigns for **females aged 26–45**
- Promote high-value product categories like **Clothing** and **Food & Beverage**
- Introduce **card-based offers** to accelerate digital payment adoption
